About

I'm a builder who treats interfaces the way a craftsman treats furniture — every joint should hold, every surface should feel right under the hand. My work moves between structured front-end engineering and the quieter, slower decisions of visual design: spacing, rhythm, restraint.

Over the past few years I've worked across web platforms, product design systems, and small studio collaborations — always chasing the point where something looks effortless because the hard part was already solved underneath.
