Our story

Named after the person who wouldn't settle for one cuisine

Aftab's Food Court started with a simple complaint: why should a group of hungry people have to agree on one restaurant? So instead, we built seven kitchens under one roof — each run by someone who's spent years perfecting a single thing.

No fusion, no shortcuts. Just the food each stall does best, cooked fresh, served fast, and eaten at long communal tables where everyone's tray looks a little different.

The idea

One roof, seven kitchens

Every stall at Aftab's is independently run, so the biryani cook never has to also be good at ice cream — and neither has to compromise.

How Aftab's works

One token, any stall

Load a single card at entry and spend it at every counter.

Under 10 minutes

Most stalls cook to order and fire the moment you pay.

Long shared tables

Seating is communal, so your table can hold seven different meals.

New special weekly

Each stall rotates one seasonal dish in, on Fridays.
